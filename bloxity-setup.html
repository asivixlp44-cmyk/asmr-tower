Description
ASMR Tower is a relaxing tower obby filled with satisfying slime, squishy and keyboard click sounds. Climb up the inside of a giant tower: wobbly jelly stairs, clicky keyboard bridges, squishy jelly domes, trusses and lime slime pools, waxed salted-butter blocks, pop-it paths, slime trampolines, sliding bubble wrap, typing keys, melting jelly, spinning mochi and the Rainbow Summit. Every surface has its own satisfying sound. Every stage is harder than the last, from EASY to INSANE, and every checkpoint pays more Wins the higher you climb. Spend your Wins on cute squishy pets that multiply your Wins, trails, spins and gear like the Speed Coil, Grappling Hook, Jetpack and the Magic Carpet (no in-app purchases: everything is earned with Wins). Race your friends to the top!
